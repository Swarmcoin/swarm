## Rules

- The sender is the person named in owner item 318, from swarmofficial@atomicmail.io; nothing goes out before the owner's word for each batch.
- Plain text, no attachments; the release is pasted below the signature.
- Two follow-ups, then stop (this replaces the old "one follow-up, then stop"). Log every send and every reply in `tracker.csv`.
- Answer every reporter's question in writing within 24 hours, including the hostile ones.
- Never offer anything in return for coverage.
- Never discuss what SWM is worth: the project does not promise a price.
- Never claim an audit: no independent audit of the SWARM-specific changes has been published, and we say exactly that.
- Paid placements are labelled as such by the outlet and never presented by us as coverage.
- Every mail that mentions mining carries, at its foot:

No coins are promised.
Please keep ASICs and rented hash power off the network.

## What to expect

Honest expectation for a new coin with no listing: two to five pieces in crypto trade media
from the free pitches, one or two FOSS write-ups about the browser and messenger, a podcast
or two in the privacy niche, and an upstream-community thread that is critical but even-handed
if we are. Mainstream tech press is unlikely without a surveillance story. A wire service buys
placement in press-release sections, not newsroom coverage; it is worth it on 1 November if
the budget allows, not before.
