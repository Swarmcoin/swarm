## Money has become a record of you. SWARM is private money you can mine yourself, and public mining opens on 1 November.

### A proof-of-work privacy coin, a messenger that pays inside the chat and a browser with the wallet built in. Experimental, open source, candid about what is unfinished.

*26 October 2026* — Every card payment, bank transfer and payment-app transaction is a record:
who paid whom, how much, when, and often where. A public blockchain is worse: it shows every
payment to everyone, forever. SWARM (ticker SWM) was built against that record. Its shielded
payments keep the sender, the receiver and the amount encrypted on its own proof-of-work
blockchain, while every node still verifies them. The mainnet has run since 2 October 2026,
15:42 UTC. On 1 November 2026, 15:42 UTC, mining opens to everyone.

**What is live today.** SWARM Wallet (Windows, macOS, Linux, Android) sends and receives SWM,
shielded by default. SWARM Messenger (Windows, macOS, Linux) carries end-to-end encrypted
messages between SWARM wallets, with payments inside the chat and sign-in with the wallet's 24
words instead of a phone number. SWARM Browser (Windows pre-release) is built from the
open-source Chromium code with Google's services removed and the wallet in the toolbar. Every
download carries its SHA-256 checksum.

**What is unfinished.** No independent audit of the SWARM-specific changes has been published.
Builds are not yet signed with a vendor certificate. The browser has no tracker blocking yet;
the messenger has no phone app. Shielding protects what is written to the chain, not the fact
that a computer connects to the network.

**Where the coins come from.** SWM is issued only by mining: a block about every 75 seconds,
6.25 SWM per block, halving every 1,680,000 blocks, at most 20,999,987.3152 SWM. The genesis
block holds no spendable coins: no premine, no sale. Every block pays 80% to its miner and 20%
to three published project addresses for the life of the chain: 8% Core Development, 4% Grants
& Ecosystem, 8% Community & Development Reserve. The split is fixed in the genesis rules and
visible in every block: a permanent allocation, paid in public.

**The closed start.** Until 31 October 2026, 15:42 UTC only the project's own machines mine:
about 33,408 blocks and about 208,800 SWM, about 0.99% of the maximum supply, each block split
like every other. The public waiting list mines for the following 24 hours; then anyone can.
The closed start is documented block by block in the explorer. No coins are promised.

**Mining.** The proof of work is Equihash 200,9. At the opening the project publishes SWARM
Node for Windows, Linux and macOS (Apple silicon and Intel): it verifies every block from
genesis and mines on the CPU while the user has pressed Start. Specialised hardware for this
proof of work exists and nothing in the rules keeps larger miners out.
Please keep ASICs and rented hash power off the network.

**Quote.** "Most digital money is a public record of who paid whom, how much and when. We built
the opposite, on shielded-payment code that has protected real money for years, and we changed
nothing in it. What we add is a network, an allocation we describe exactly as it is, and the
apps people need to use private money: a wallet, a messenger and a browser. We would rather be
believed for what we admit than praised for what we claim." — the SWARM founding team
