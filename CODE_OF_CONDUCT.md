# Code of conduct

SWARM is built by people who disagree about many things and agree on one: everyone deserves private money. We keep the project a place where that work gets done.

## Expected

- Be direct and be kind. Criticise code, designs and claims; do not attack people.
- Assume good faith; ask before you conclude.
- Welcome newcomers. The question you find obvious was not obvious once.
- Keep discussions on topic and in the open, so others can learn from them.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, slurs, sexualised language or imagery, doxxing.
- Deliberate disruption: derailing, spamming, sock-puppet accounts, brigading.
- Advertising other coins, pumping prices or soliciting investment in project spaces.
- Posting other people's private data, keys or recovery phrases, including your own.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, the project's social accounts and any event where someone represents SWARM.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content and block accounts that break these rules, and will explain why. Report problems to `swarmofficial@atomicmail.io`; reports are read by the maintainers and kept confidential as far as the law allows.
