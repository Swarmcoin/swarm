## Anyone can now mine the privacy coin that keeps payments off the record. SWARM opened public mining at 15:42 UTC today.

### The 30-day closed start ended on schedule; the first public blocks are in the explorer; SWARM Node is published for Windows, Linux and macOS with its checksum. Experimental, open source, candid about what is unfinished.

*1 November 2026* — For most of history, paying someone left no record beyond the two people
involved. Cards, bank apps and public blockchains ended that: every payment is logged, and the
log belongs to someone else. SWARM (SWM) is a proof-of-work cryptocurrency built to bring the
older property back: shielded payments whose sender, receiver and amount stay encrypted on the
chain while the network still verifies them. At 15:42 UTC today SWARM ended its 30-day closed
start and opened mining to everyone. The first public block, number [HEIGHT-OPEN], is visible
to anyone at mainnet.explore.swarm.green.

**What opened today.** SWARM Node, a desktop application for Windows, Linux and macOS (Apple
silicon and Intel), is published at swarm.green/ecosystem with its SHA-256 checksum and a
mining guide. It runs a full node, verifies every block from the genesis block before it
mines, and mines on the CPU for as long as the user has pressed Start, leaving one core free.
The rules are the same for everyone: no admin key, no checkpoint, no way to create a coin
without finding a block. Rewards can be spent after 100 blocks.

**What the closed start produced, as mined.** SWARM mainnet has been running since 2 October
2026, 15:42 UTC. Until 31 October 2026, 15:42 UTC only the project's own machines mined; the
closed start ended at block [HEIGHT-CLOSE]. The project had estimated about 33,408 blocks and
about 208,800 SWM, about 0.99% of the maximum supply; the figures as mined are in the explorer,
block by block, each split like every other block. For the 24 hours before today's opening the
[N] people on the public waiting list received the node software. No coins were promised to
anyone, and none are now.

**Where every block goes.** 6.25 SWM per block, a block about every 75 seconds, halving every
1,680,000 blocks, at most 20,999,987.3152 SWM: the same shape as Bitcoin's supply, and for the
same reason. Every block pays 80% to the miner who found it and 20% to three published project
addresses for the whole life of the chain: 8% Core Development, 4% Grants & Ecosystem, 8%
Community & Development Reserve. Fixed in the genesis rules, visible in every block, not a
premine; the project describes it as a permanent allocation and the closed start as a closed
start.

**What is unfinished, in the project's own words.** SWARM is experimental software. No
independent audit of the SWARM-specific changes has been published. Builds are not yet signed
with a vendor certificate. The browser is a Windows pre-release without tracker blocking; the
messenger is a first release without a phone app. Shielding protects what is written to the
chain, not the fact that a computer connects to the network.

**On hardware.** The proof of work is Equihash 200,9. Specialised hardware for it exists, and
nothing in the rules keeps larger miners out; the project does not promise that home computers
stay competitive, and says so rather than pretend otherwise.
Please keep ASICs and rented hash power off the network.
