# Forum, social and listing plan

The research file (`../research/community-and-forums-2026-10.md`) says which venues tolerate a new coin and under what conditions. This file turns that into a plan with dates. The posts themselves are in this folder: the Bitcointalk ANN (`bitcointalk-ann.md`), the introduction for forum.zcashcommunity.com (the `*-community-forum-intro.md` file) and the Show HN, social and Lemmy texts (`show-hn-and-short-posts.md`).

Rules that apply everywhere: every post in a community that is not ours begins with "I work on SWARM."; our own social accounts speak as "we"; story first, the product at most one post in five, never two in a row; say what is unfinished before the product facts; link only to swarm.green, mainnet.explore.swarm.green and github.com/Swarmcoin; never discuss what SWM is worth, never mention the token on Base; never argue; every mention of mining or the waiting list carries "No coins are promised.", and every mining call carries "Please keep ASICs and rented hash power off the network."; security reports get the SECURITY.md path and nothing else.
