# PR outreach

What is here: two press releases (`press-release-1-mainnet-live.md` for 7 October,
`press-release-2-mining-open.md` for 1 November, with two blanks to fill at send time), the
media list with priorities and angles (`media-list.csv`), five pitch templates by segment
(`pitch-emails.md`) and an empty tracker (`outreach-tracker.csv`).

## The sequence

| Day | Do |
| --- | --- |
| 7 Oct | Send release 1 to every priority-1 contact with template A or B. Post the Zcash forum intro (`../forums/`) and send template D to ZecHub and Zingo Labs the same day, so nobody in the Zcash community hears about us from a journalist first. |
| 8 to 10 Oct | Priority-2 crypto and privacy media. Submit the CoinGecko preview, CoinMarketCap untracked, CoinPaprika and Blockspot forms (checklist in `../forums/README.md`). |
| 14 to 16 Oct | Podcasts and YouTubers (template C). One follow-up to week-1 pitches with no reply. |
| 21 to 24 Oct | Priority-3 tech media; the Monero-side podcasts, knowing they will be adversarial. |
| 25 to 30 Oct | Pools and MiningPoolStats (template E). Prepare release 2: waiting-list count, final closed-start block height. |
| 1 Nov, 16:00 UTC | Release 2 to everyone who replied or published, then to the rest of the list. Optional: Chainwire or U.Today for guaranteed placement. |
| 3 to 6 Nov | Thank everyone who covered it; send corrections if anything was reported wrong; log results. |

## Rules

- Send from swarmofficial@atomicmail.io. Plain text, release pasted below the signature.
- Never offer anything in return for coverage. Never discuss price. Never say "audited".
- Answer every reporter's question in writing within 24 hours, including the hostile ones;
  the FAQ at the bottom of `../reddit/01-privacycoins-launch-writeup.md` is the source.
- One follow-up, then stop. Log every send and every reply.
- Paid placements are labelled as such by the outlet and never presented by us as coverage.

## What to expect

Honest expectation for a new coin with no listing: two to five pieces in crypto trade media
from the free pitches, one or two FOSS write-ups about the browser and messenger, a podcast
or two in the privacy niche, and a Zcash-community thread that is critical but fair if we
are. Mainstream tech press is unlikely without a surveillance story. A wire service buys
placement in press-release sections, not newsroom coverage; it is worth it on 1 November if
the budget allows, not before.
