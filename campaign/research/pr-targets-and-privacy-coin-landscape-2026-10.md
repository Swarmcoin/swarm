Themes: (1) two surviving mandatory-privacy L1s (Monero, Pirate Chain) against the
optional-privacy bloc (Zcash, Dash, Firo, Litecoin, Decred); capital in 2025 and 2026 went
to Zcash because optionality kept it listable. (2) Zcash's shielded pool technology is
becoming the standard library: Dash adopted Orchard, Pirate is testing it, SWARM inherits
it unmodified. (3) Pivots and consolidation: Horizen, Secret, Aztec, Railgun, Oasis are
platforms, not coins. (4) Launches without a premine or sale are rare: Monero, Grin, Pirate,
Litecoin, and SWARM; Zcash, Zano, Decred, Beam, Firo and Dash all carry founder or treasury
allocations. SWARM carries a permanent 20% block-reward allocation and says so; it does not
claim a fair launch without allocation.

## Regulation

- **EU AMLR (Regulation (EU) 2024/1624), Article 79**: credit and financial institutions
  and CASPs may not keep anonymous accounts, including accounts allowing anonymisation or
  obfuscation "including through anonymity-enhancing coins". **Applies from 10 July 2027**
  (not 1 July, as often reported). It removes EU fiat on- and off-ramps for such assets; it
  does not criminalise self-custody or peer-to-peer use. MiCA already triggered regional
  delistings. The AMLA will issue guidance on which assets count; optional-privacy coins'
  status is contested.
- **FATF**, 7th targeted update (16 July 2026): 83% of jurisdictions have Travel Rule
  legislation; stablecoins now carry about 84% of illicit virtual-asset volume; supervision
  of anonymity-enhancing products still urged.
- **Japan**: exchanges delisted XMR, ZEC and DASH under FSA pressure since 2018. **South
  Korea**: ban on high-risk coins for VASPs since March 2021. **Dubai (VARA)**: issuance and
  VASP activity in anonymity-enhanced cryptocurrencies prohibited since 2023.
- **United States**: no ban; ZEC trades on Coinbase and Robinhood. Enforcement targets
  operators: Tornado Cash sanctions lifted 21 March 2025; Roman Storm convicted only of
  unlicensed money transmission (Aug 2025); Samourai founders sentenced (Nov 2025). Pending:
  Blockchain Regulatory Certainty Act (passed Senate Banking, May 2026), CLARITY Act (passed
  the House).
